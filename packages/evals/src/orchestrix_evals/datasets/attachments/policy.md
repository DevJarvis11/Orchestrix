# Expense policy (excerpt)

1. Meals while travelling: up to $60 per day, receipts required above $25.
2. Hotels: up to $220 per night in tier-1 cities, $150 elsewhere.
3. Flights: economy for trips under 6 hours; premium economy above 6 hours.
4. Equipment over $500 needs manager approval before purchase.
5. Claims must be filed within 30 days of the expense.
