# Acme Notes app: product notes (Q3)

- 12,400 weekly active users; 8% week-over-week growth since the July redesign.
- Top requests: offline mode (31% of feedback), shared notebooks (24%), dark mode (18%).
- Churn is concentrated in the first week: 41% of new users never create a second note.
- The iOS app crashes on launch for 0.7% of sessions on iOS 18.0 only (fixed in 3.4.1).
- Pricing: Free, Plus ($4/month), Team ($9/user/month). Team is 22% of revenue.
